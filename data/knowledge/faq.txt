Inventory Chatbot FAQ

What can the assistant do?
- Search products, explain product information, check current stock, identify low-stock items, and provide relevant documentation.

Where does stock information come from?
- Current stock is read from the live inventory database/API. Documentation and RAG must never be treated as the source of truth for quantities.

What if a product is out of stock?
- The assistant should state that it is out of stock and can suggest checking related products or creating a reorder request when the user has permission.

What if the assistant does not know an answer?
- It should say that the information is unavailable instead of inventing an answer.
