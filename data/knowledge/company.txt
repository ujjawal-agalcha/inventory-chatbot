Company Knowledge

This knowledge base contains information about the company's
IoT hardware inventory system.

The system manages IoT hardware components and electronic
devices.

The inventory contains products such as ESP32 modules,
Arduino boards, motor drivers, motors, sensors, batteries,
displays, relays, communication modules, and electronic
components.

ESP32-CAM is an ESP32-based camera development board used
for projects involving wireless connectivity and imaging.

Arduino Uno R3 is a microcontroller development board
commonly used for electronics and embedded-system projects.

The inventory database is responsible for storing current
stock quantities. Current stock information must be obtained
from the inventory system rather than from this knowledge base.
